Questions

Support questions.

Message us any time.

Can you help if you didn’t install our system?

Yes. We regularly tune and upgrade systems installed by others, in person or remotely.

How does remote tuning work?

We connect to your digital mixer using specialised tuning software and adjust EQ, levels and feedback while someone at the masjid listens and gives feedback.

Do you follow up after an install?

Yes. We follow up after some time to make sure everything is working as it should.

Can you help with a temporary or Ramadan prayer hall?

Yes, we provide portable systems for temporary prayer spaces.
