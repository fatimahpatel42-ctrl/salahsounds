What's included

A system built around your building.

Domes, tiled floors and bare walls fight every word. Every part of the system is chosen and tuned for your hall.

3-tier mic system

Separate microphones for Qira'at, Ruku & Sujood and the khutbah, each with its own EQ so every part of the salah sounds right.

Haramain EQ tuning

Full EQ tuning with specialised software, feedback elimination and our custom Haramain sound settings.

Digital mixer & presets

Saved presets for adhan, khutbah and tarawih. One button, no guesswork for your volunteers.

Speakers that reach everyone
